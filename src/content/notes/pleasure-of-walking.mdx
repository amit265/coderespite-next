---
title: 'The Simple Pleasure of Walking'
date: '2026-03-13'
---

I've started taking 20-minute walks without my phone. No podcasts, no music, just the sound of the neighborhood. It's amazing how many details you notice when you're not distracted—the way the light hits the trees, the specific shade of a neighbor's front door. Highly recommend it for clearing the mental cobwebs.
