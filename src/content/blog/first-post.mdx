---
title: 'Why I am Building This Notebook'
date: '2026-01-12'
---

I've decided to build this public notebook for a few reasons.

First, I need a place to think. Writing is the best way for me to clarify my own ideas. If I can't explain something simply, I probably don't understand it well enough.

Second, I want a record of my work and learning that is not tied to any platform. This is my own space.

Finally, I want to move away from the "look at me" culture of tech. This isn't a portfolio or a marketing site. It's just a notebook. If it's useful to others, that's a bonus.
